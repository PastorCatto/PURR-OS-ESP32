Type Wheel

The Type shell's list screen becomes the whole shell: one column, one highlight, drill down and back. A click wheel sits on the right where the board has a touch screen. The pad and trackball keep the full-width list. Everything below works: click the screen, then use the arrow keys, or drag the ring.

Click the screen to give it the keys: ▲▼ scroll · Enter A · Esc B · H Y · Space FUNC · ◀▶ track. The mouse wheel scrolls it too.

Kept from the Type shell

  • Paper, ink and one accent that follows the Personalization setting; the text on the accent flips between ink and paper by contrast.
  • Space Grotesk for names, Space Mono for labels. Both are already bitmap fonts in tpfonts.
  • The list is the board's app list: 62 px rows, a chevron on each, the chosen row inverted with an accent chevron, the ink hint bar with the keys in the accent.
  • The side panel stays, and keeps the stacked clock on Home. It now shows one fact about the highlighted row (cover, count, value) in the place of an iPod's cover preview.

Taken from the click wheel

  • One highlight, always a drill-down: a row pushes the next list in from the right, Back slides it out. The title bar names where you are.
  • Now Playing: the wheel sets the volume, the centre switches it to scrub (the bar grows a playhead), and the same three taps work from any list.
  • One row per 20° of travel, about 18 per turn. Rows clamp at the ends and do not wrap.
  • A "Shuffle Songs" row on Home, and the playing track marked with a triangle in the song lists.

No new buttons on the pad

The Type controls board already has the wheel's whole vocabulary: ◀ ▶ for previous and next, FUNC tap for play and pause, A to select, B for back, Y for home. The only change is that ▲ ▼ become the wheel's scroll, and in Now Playing set the volume. X keeps its job as the options menu for the screen.

Home loses its three shortcut tiles. FUNC held plus A, B, X or Y can still jump to the four pinned items. Whether those live as rows or stay tiles is a question below.

How it would be built

  • The rows, scroll bar and panel are what purr_tp_home.c already draws for the app list. The draft makes that list the shell's navigation and adds one column.
  • The wheel is drawn from primitives: a ring, 36 ticks, a disc and four glyphs. No images, so nothing extra in PSRAM, and one small invalidated square per tick.
  • Touch angle from atan2 of the touch point to the wheel's centre, one step per 20° of change. The step size is the thing to tune on the device.
  • Layout B is drawn only when touch is the active input. Pad and trackball keep layout A.
  • On the 2.8 inch T-Deck panel the 232 px wheel here is 116 px across, about 21 mm, so the ring is a band of roughly 6 mm. A thumb fits. It is tighter than a real wheel, which is the first thing to check on hardware.
  • Nothing here is written in C or built into an image.

Questions for you

  • Should the wheel be a setting (Settings, Wheel, as in the mockup), or appear by itself when the last input was a touch?
  • Home as a list with the clock in the side panel, or keep the big clock and tiles and use the wheel list from Apps downward?
  • A wider wheel (136 px at 1×) leaves a 184 px list and about 5 characters fewer per row. Is the bigger target worth that?
  • Should B held go home, like Menu held on a click wheel, instead of Y alone?
